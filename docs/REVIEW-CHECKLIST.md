# Review checklist (for the owner)

Every training is a **draft** until you set `status: reviewed` in its file (or ask me to do it after you say "reviewed"). Production builds show only reviewed trainings.

## How to review one training (about 15 minutes each)

1. Open the preview link. Open the training under *Verstehen*, read it.
2. Do every task under *Trainieren* once. Make one mistake on purpose in a few tasks to see the feedback.
3. Mark your answers below (✓ fine, ✎ change, ? unsure) and send me the list. I make the changes.

For every training ask yourself:
- Is the German correct and natural? Is it at the right level (nothing too hard without a gloss)?
- Are the rules true, without wrong simplifications? Are important exceptions mentioned?
- Are all correct answers accepted? Is any wrong answer accidentally accepted?
- Is the German simple and warm enough for 10-year-olds?
- Do the examples avoid stereotypes and real people?

## Training by training

| Training | Level | Special questions |
|---|---|---|
| Cases: overview (`cases-overview`) | A0 | Are the verb and preposition lists right as examples? Is "A0" a good label? |
| haben and sein (`haben-sein-present`) | A1 | Check "Zwei Verben für jeden Tag", "Die Verb-Werkstatt"; the note under the sein table. |
| Regular verbs (`regular-verbs-present`) | A1 | Check the verb list and the rule boxes. |
| Sentence structure (`sentence-structure-a1`) | A1 | Do you accept "In Berlin wohne ich." at A1? Time words heute, morgen, jetzt: is "Morgen wohne ich in Berlin." too odd? |
| Modal verbs (`modal-verbs-present`) | A1 | Meaning texts, especially **sollen**. |
| Verbs with vowel change (`vowel-change-verbs`) | A1 | Missing verbs: treffen, waschen, tragen, wissen. Add some? |
| Separable verbs (`separable-verbs-present`) | A1 | Example sentences per verb: do they sound natural? |
| Main clauses: short sentences (`main-clauses-a1`) | A1 | Is a maximum of 5 sentence parts right? Roles shown: who, when, where, what. |
| Akkusativ (`accusative-a1`) | A1 | Definite-article drills use masculine nouns only. Pronoun list. |
| Perfekt (`perfekt`) | A2 | Your own training: check that it is close to your original. |
| Dativ (`dative-a2`) | A2 | Scope: verbs, prepositions, plural -n. Left out: es dir, two-way prepositions. |
| Two-way prepositions (`two-way-prepositions-a2`) | A2 | Wo? / wohin? as leading idea. Left out: hängen, abstract uses. |
| Main clauses: longer sentences (`main-clauses-a2`) | A2 | Usual order: subject, time, Dativ, Akkusativ, place. Marked orders are not accepted. |
| Genitiv (`genitive-b1`) | B1 | Is B1 the right label? Only -es is taught (des Mannes), not des Manns. Brief note on des Jungen. |
| Reading A1, A2, B1 (`reading-a1`, `reading-a2`, `reading-b1`) | A1–B1 | Texts at the right level? Statements fair? Short answers: is a correct answer rejected? |

More detail on each point is in `docs/CONTENT-QUESTIONS.md`.
